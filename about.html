Who we are

Care guided by faith. Grounded in compassion.

FEMBOD Faith Home is a clinic serving people and families in Ota and Iju-Ota, Ogun State. Our purpose is to make it easier to find information, ask questions and take the next step toward care.

Our approach

Care begins with how we treat people.

A welcoming experience should never be an extra. It is part of how people feel heard, respected and supported.

We believe every person deserves dignity, thoughtful communication and clear guidance. Whether you are contacting us for yourself or someone in your family, we encourage you to ask questions and confirm the details that matter before visiting.

  • Listen with patience and respect
  • Share clear, practical information
  • Help visitors identify the appropriate branch
  • Be transparent about the services available

Useful information

Before you visit, here are a few answers.

If your question is not covered below, contact the clinic directly. For urgent or life-threatening symptoms, seek emergency care immediately.

Where is FEMBOD Faith Home located?

FEMBOD Faith Home has two locations: Elejigbo Estate, behind Obasanjo Farm Road, Ota; and Bankole Junction / Bus Stop, Idiroko Road, Iju-Ota, Ogun State. Visit the Locations page and select “Find directions” for your preferred branch.

What are the opening hours?

FEMBOD Faith Home is listed as open 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Contact the team before visiting if you have a specific enquiry.

What medical services do you provide?

Contact FEMBOD Faith Home directly for the latest information about available medical services.

Do both branches offer the same services?

Service availability may differ by location. Contact the team to find out which branch can assist you.

Does FEMBOD Faith Home provide nursing training?

Visit the Professional Development page or contact FEMBOD Faith Home for information about current learning opportunities, eligibility and supervision.

How do I get directions?

Visit the Locations page and select “Find directions” for your preferred branch. You can also contact the clinic if you need help.

What should I do in a medical emergency?

If someone may be in immediate danger or has severe symptoms, contact an appropriate emergency service or go to the nearest suitable emergency facility. Do not delay urgent care while waiting for a website response.
